Approach

Analysis is where good student research stalls.

Most student projects are not let down by the question or the fieldwork. They are let down at the point where a spreadsheet of raw responses has to become a defensible results chapter — the wrong test chosen because it was the one available, a table that a reviewer sends back, a figure that cannot be read once it is printed.

Stat Easy exists to take that step seriously without pricing it out of reach. Every test is chosen for the data and the objectives, written up so it can go straight into a methods section, and explained until the researcher can answer for it without help.

Specimen · illustrative text

Statistical analysis — a paragraph written to drop into your methods section

Data were entered into a spreadsheet, checked for completeness and analysed using standard statistical software. Continuous variables are summarised as mean ± standard deviation and categorical variables as frequencies with percentages. The Shapiro–Wilk test was used to assess normality of distribution. Continuous variables were compared across the three dietary groups using one-way analysis of variance, with the Kruskal–Wallis test applied where the assumption of normality was not met. Categorical variables were compared using Pearson's chi-square test. A two-sided p-value below 0.05 was taken as statistically significant.

The software named in your own paragraph is set to whatever your department requires. Written so you can describe your own methods accurately, in your own viva.

Questions

      
What students ask before they commit.

    

    

      

        What do you need from me to start?
        
Your objectives, your study design and your data. A spreadsheet is ideal, but a scan of a paper proforma or a form export works too. If nothing has been collected yet, get in touch earlier rather than later — the decisions taken before data collection are the ones that save a study.

      

      

        How long does it take?
        
It depends on the size of the dataset and how much cleaning it needs. The delivery date is agreed with you before work starts, so give us your submission deadline up front — if it cannot honestly be met, we say so rather than take the project.

      

      

        Will I be able to explain the results myself?
        
That is the point of the last stage. Every project ends with a walkthrough of what each test was for and what the numbers mean, in plain language. Nobody should be standing in front of an examiner reading out a figure they do not understand.

      

      

        Is this ethical? Isn't the analysis my own work?
        
Statistical support is a normal and declarable part of research — the same role a statistician plays on any published paper. Stat Easy handles the analysis and the output; the study, the interpretation and the writing remain yours, and the methods are always explained so you can describe them accurately.

      

      

        What if my guide asks for changes?
        
Revisions within the agreed scope are included. If your guide asks for an additional analysis that was not among the original objectives, it is quoted separately before anything is done.

      

      

        Why only five projects a month?
        
Because Stat Easy is run by a student who is also finishing a degree, and a rushed analysis is worse than none. Five is what can be done properly. Slots for a given month tend to go early, so ask before the deadline is close.
